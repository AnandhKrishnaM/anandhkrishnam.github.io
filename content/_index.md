I am a PhD student in mathematical relativity at the Department of Mathematical Sciences, Chalmers University of Technology and the University of Gothenburg, supervised by Thomas Bäckdahl. I work on the stability of black-hole spacetimes.

Before coming to Chalmers I completed the BS‑MS programme at IISER Pune. My master's thesis, carried out at the Max Planck Institute for Gravitational Physics (AEI) in Potsdam and at IUCAA in Pune, was on null infinity, the BMS group and gravitational memory, supervised by Sajad Aghapour and the late Naresh Dadhich.

This site collects my research, lecture notes and notes on mathematics, physics and philosophy.
